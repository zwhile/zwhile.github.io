I'm an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Computer Science and Information Systems](https://academics.ysu.edu/computer-science-and-information-systems) (CSIS) at [Youngstown State University](https://ysu.edu/).  I earned my MS and PhD in Computer Science at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.cics.umass.edu/) in 2025 under the advisement of Dr. Ali Sarvghad.  Before that, I completed my BS in [Computer Science](https://academics.ysu.edu/computer-science-and-information-systems) and [Mathematics](https://academics.ysu.edu/mathematics-and-statistics) at Youngstown State University in 2018.

My research primarily lies at the **intersection of data visualization and aging**, with a focus on empirical studies investigating the perceptual impacts of various visualization design choices.  I am also interested in topics in broader Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), including **accessibility** and **social computing**.  Additionally, I have experience in data science, machine learning, deep learning, and computer graphics.

In my free time I enjoy playing board/video games, watching anime, playing the euphonium/tuba, and playing disc golf.
