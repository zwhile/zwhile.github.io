Hello!  I'm Zack, a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Systems (CSIS) at Youngstown State University.  I earned my MS and PhD in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst in September 2025 under the advisement of Dr. Ali Sarvghad, and I completed my BS in Computer Science and Mathematics at Youngstown State University in 2018.

My research primarily lies at the intersection of data visualization and aging, with a focus on empirical studies investigating the perceptual impacts of various visualization design choices.  I also do some work in broader Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), including accessibility and social computing.  I also have experience in machine learning, deep learning, and computer graphics.
